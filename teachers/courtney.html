Faculty

Courtney Donofrio

Integrative Nutrition Health Coach

Courtney Donofrio is a Certified Integrative Nutrition Health Coach and the founder of EatBiohackLove, a longevity coaching and lifestyle brand dedicated to making the science of living longer and better accessible to everyone.

Based in Amsterdam and originally from the United States, Courtney brings a truly global perspective to the world of wellness. Her path into longevity was forged through her own transformative health journey: after years of unexplained digestive issues, hormonal disruption, and skin changes, she discovered that chronic mold and mycotoxin exposure was silently undermining her health. That experience ignited a deep passion for functional medicine, root cause healing, and the kind of proactive self advocacy that the modern medical system rarely teaches us.

Trained through the Institute for Integrative Nutrition (IIN), one of the world's leading health coaching programs, Courtney specializes in gut health, hormone balance, detoxification, and biohacking for longevity. Her approach empowers clients to stop being passive passengers in their own health and become the CEOs of their wellbeing: asking better questions, reading their own bodies, and building sustainable habits that compound over a lifetime.

As a founding instructor at Longevity Life Academy with eTeacher Group, Courtney brings the same clarity, warmth, and science backed insight to the global classroom that she brings to her private clients. Her mission is simple: longevity should not be a luxury for the elite. It should be a toolkit everyone can pick up and use, starting today.
