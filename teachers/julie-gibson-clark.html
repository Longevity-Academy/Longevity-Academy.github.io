A Day In Her Protocol

Simple. Consistent. Repeatable.

No exotic hardware, no seven-figure lab — just a rhythm she can keep for decades. This is the exact kind of routine she teaches at the Academy.

5:00 AM

Rise & center

Wakes before the house does — reads, journals and meditates with green tea in hand. “The quietest, most peaceful part of the day.”

7:00 AM

Move & sweat

Strength work, a Norwegian 4×4 for VO₂ max, then sauna and a cold shower. The trick: “Just go for the sauna. That’s all you have to do today.”

Late morning

Eat the rainbow

A big greens drink, a warm greens latte, then roughly a pound of colorful vegetables with protein grazed across the day.

1:00 PM

20 minutes of calm

Transcendental Meditation, a daily practice since 2019. Her most surprising benefit? Deeper, steadier sleep.

Early evening

Dinner & connection

An early meal — ideally about three hours before bed — built around time with her son, not just macros.

9:00 PM

Guard the sleep

An evening walk, then lights out. “You cannot biohack your way out of a good night’s sleep.”
