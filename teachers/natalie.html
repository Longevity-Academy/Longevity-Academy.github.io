Natalie Blackbourne

Longevity Researcher & Educator

Natalie Blackbourne is a researcher, biohacker, and educator passionate about helping people optimize their health, energy, and cognitive performance through science-backed, practical strategies. She specializes in translating complex topics in longevity, neuroscience, nutrition, and wellness into engaging, approachable education that empowers sustainable lifestyle change.

Her background in emotional intelligence shapes how we perceive and integrate aspects of ourselves on a biological level.

Recent Activity in the Longevity & Wellness Field

Over the past several years, Natalie has focused extensively on researching longevity, nutritional neuroscience, cognitive optimization, emotional well-being, metabolic health, and holistic wellness. Her aim is not only to extend lifespans, but to embrace a mind-body connection that allows for deep personal satisfaction with the goal of a truly well-lived life.

Passion & Teaching Philosophy

Natalie is deeply passionate about empowering people to better understand their bodies, brains, and long-term health potential. Her teaching approach combines scientific rigor with warmth, curiosity, and real-world application, creating an engaging learning environment where students feel informed, capable, and inspired to take ownership of their well-being.
