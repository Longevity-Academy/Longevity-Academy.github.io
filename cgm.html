A 14-day biosensor that reads your glucose in real time, so you can finally see how your body responds to food, sleep, training, and stress. The Lingo biosensor plus full Lingo app access are included with every Longevity Life Academy enrollment.

How it works

STEP 01

Apply

Place the biosensor on the back of your upper arm. Painless, single-use applicator.

STEP 02

Pair

Open the Lingo app on your phone and pair the sensor in seconds. Full app access is included.

STEP 03

Track

See your glucose patterns for 14 days. Bring the data to your cohort.
