Method and limits

All figures above are read from the Longevity Life Academy store pixel (dataset 1440305917310328), comparing 15 Sep 18:00 – 16 Sep 13:07 IDT against the preceding 14 Sep 06:00 – 15 Sep 18:00.

One honest limitation. The pixel strips query strings, so it cannot split organic from paid by itself, and paid volume was not held constant across the two windows. Per-visitor intent rates are therefore directional, not a controlled experiment. Every post link does carry its own per-group UTM, so the exact organic split is recoverable in GA4; that report is pending an account sign-in and will be issued as a correction if it contradicts anything here. No blended figure is being presented as organic.
